Featured Adventures

Los Dientes del Diablo

Our most demanding run, "The Devil's Teeth" throws you straight into a churning stretch of Class IV and V whitewater. Experienced paddlers will love the technical lines, big drops, and non-stop action through the narrow canyon.

  • Duration: Full day (7 hours)
  • Difficulty: Class IV-V
  • Distance: 18 miles
  • Price: $125 per person

Big Mallard

A classic half-day adventure featuring rolling Class III waves and plenty of splash. Big Mallard is the perfect introduction to whitewater for active beginners and returning rafters looking for a fun, fast-paced morning.

  • Duration: Half day (4 hours)
  • Difficulty: Class III
  • Distance: 8 miles
  • Price: $75 per person

Half Day Adventure

Our family favourite. Glide through calm pools and gentle Class II riffles while your guide shares stories about the river valley. Ideal for children, first-timers, and anyone who wants a relaxed taste of rafting.

  • Duration: Half day (3 hours)
  • Difficulty: Class II
  • Distance: 6 miles
  • Price: $55 per person

Rapid Thunder

Big water, big waves, and a legendary rapids section that thunders for a full mile. Rapid Thunder pairs adrenaline with stunning scenery and a riverside lunch stop, making it a favourite full-day getaway.

  • Duration: Full day (6 hours)
  • Difficulty: Class IV
  • Distance: 14 miles
  • Price: $110 per person
